What just happened?
agent flow
  1. 1
    The shopper clicks “Negotiate with AI”.

    The widget opens /negotiate.html?merchant=m1&product=p1 in a new tab. No storefront replatform, no checkout rewrite.

  2. 2
    Two agents negotiate in the open.

    ProcureBot proposes; the Northwind Seller Agent counters inside the merchant’s published policy. The model writes the words — the server fixes every number, and the private sell floor never leaves the server.

  3. 3
    A signed contract locks the price.

    Both agents sign a contract hash with fixed terms before any money moves. The storefront still checks out through PayPal at the agreed amount.

  4. 4
    Protection travels with the order.

    If delivery fails, the buyer agent files a claim and the merchant reserve refunds instantly — no chargeback queue.
