    important
    sell_min / sell_max
    Private floor and ceiling. Never exposed to the index, the buyer agent or the negotiation preview.
    publish_min / publish_max
    The public “from” range. This is what the index, storefront and preview display.

    The published policy price the seller agent may accept and the maximum discount it can offer.
